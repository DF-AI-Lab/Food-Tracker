Look at this photo of one food item. The photo may be sideways or upside down.
Focus only on the main item. Ignore anything in the background.

Return ONLY one JSON object, no other text:
{"name": "...", "sub": "...", "date": "YYYY-MM-DD", "bb": true, "price": 3.5, "packs": 2, "kind": "veg", "noDate": true}

Fields (leave out any field you don't have; never write null):
- name: the main food plus its cut or type, never the shop or brand.
  Examples: "Whole chicken", "Chicken fillets", "Chicken thighs", "Beef mince", "Smoked bacon", "Semi-skimmed milk".
  Keep a word only if you'd write it on a shopping list. Leave out shop, brand, size, weight, pack count,
  "British", "Free range", "Finest", "Organic" and "% fat" (those go in sub).
  Capital first letter, rest lower case. Plural when that's how you'd say it ("Sausages", "Eggs").
  Names already in the app: {{NAMES}}
  Use one of those only if it is exactly the same food, cut and type ("Chicken" is not "Whole chicken"). A similar food is NOT the same:
  a product made from or with a listed food is a different food (chicken nuggets are not Chicken,
  garlic bread is not Bread). Then make a new short name.
- sub: full name from the packet, with brand. Example: "Asda whole chicken".
- date: the USE BY date, or the BEST BEFORE date if there is no use by. Ignore "display until" and "sell by".
  Day and month only: use {{YEAR}}. If the year is printed, use the printed year.
  A date printed with no label (no "use by" or "best before" words) still counts: give it, with no bb.
- bb: true only when the date you gave is a best before ("Best before", "BBE", "Best before end").
- price: a number, only if a price is printed on a label. Never guess.
- packs: only when the photo shows more than one of the same pack.
- noDate: true only when the packet clearly has no date printed anywhere.
- kind: "veg" for fresh veg and fruit (potatoes, carrots, onions, apples...) with NO use by or best before printed.
  If a veg item has a use by or best before (e.g. a salad bowl), it is a normal item: give the date, no kind.
  Veg gets name, sub and kind only. Never a date: codes like "I 23" or "J 2" and "Display until" on veg are not dates.

Reading dates:
- Dates may be squashed with no spaces: "10OCT" means 10 October. A letter O can look like a zero.
- Dates may be numbers: 14/10 or 14.10.26 is day first (UK).

Never guess. If you can't read the date clearly, leave out date. A missing date is fine; a wrong date is not.
