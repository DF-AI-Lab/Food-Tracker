8. **Meal picker** still shows the old countdown words ("1 day left", "Today!") from the shared logic.
9. **Some small buttons** (Use by / Best before, Dark / Light) are 30–34px tall like the mocks, not 44px.
10. **Bottom + turns white** while on the Add page (as in the mocks).
11. **Settings → Reload app** added (mock). Backup / Restore kept in Settings under its own heading.
12. **Mic** still says "coming soon" (unchanged behaviour).
